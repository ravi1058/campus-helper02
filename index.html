⚡ How Campus Helper Works Fast

Campus Helper is engineered for instant clarity and zero bloat. Here is the fast workflow to master your semester in under 60 seconds:

⚡

1. Free Hour Finder & Timetable

Unique Twist

Select your day. The system scans class start/end times, highlights gaps $\ge 30$ mins, and delivers targeted 30m/60m/90m study sprints (flashcards, quiet library drills, or gym breaks).

🎯

2. Priorities & Exam Countdowns

Keep daily coursework in check with satisfaction checkmarks. Live ticking countdown clocks track final exams down to the second.

💳

3. Campus Finance Tracker

Log meal and transit expenses in 3 seconds. Animated category progress bars dynamically change color to prevent mid-month budget depletion.

📚

4. Study Hub & Test Center

Access verified free textbooks (MIT, Stanford, OSTEP), monitor circular course progress dials, and challenge your brain with 5-question sprint quizzes.

💡

5. Innovation Kanban & Opportunities

Move your startup ideas and hackathon prototypes across stages with one click, while tracking top tech internships and grants.
